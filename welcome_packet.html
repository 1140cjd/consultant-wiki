Welcome to Exit Factor Charlotte.

You've made a significant decision — one that puts you at the center of one of the most important conversations a business owner will ever have. The question of how and when to exit a business isn't just a financial transaction. It's the culmination of decades of sacrifice, risk, and perseverance. And you're going to help them do it right.

"Over the next decade, an estimated $10 trillion in business wealth will change hands as Baby Boomer owners retire. Most of them are unprepared. That's the problem we solve — and the opportunity in front of you."

This packet is your operating system for the first 90 days. It contains your pre-training ramp-up curriculum, your task checklist before training, a day-by-day plan for your first 30 days after training, and the KPIs you'll use to measure whether you're on the right track.

There is no shortcut to building a great practice. But there is a proven path — and this document is it. Follow it with discipline, show up with energy, and trust the process.

I'm in your corner. Let's build something great together.

How to Use This Packet

Before Training: Work through the 4-Week Ramp Up and complete every item on the Pre-Training Checklist. Arrive prepared — not just present.

During Training: Bring your List of 100, your draft elevator pitch, and 3 specific questions based on what you've learned.

After Training: Follow the 30-Day daily plan to the letter. Then shift to the Month 2–3 weekly rhythm. Track your KPIs every week without exception.

Section 01
4-Week Pre-Training Ramp Up
EXIT FACTOR
Week 1
Foundation: Know Your Company
Exit Factor Overview
Read all brand materials and the company's mission, vision, and values.
The 5-Phase Methodology
Study each phase in depth. Practice a verbal walkthrough until you can deliver it naturally to a business owner.
Ideal Client Profile
Owners with $1M–$20M revenue, 2–15 years from exit, often unaware of their value gap. Know their pain points cold.
Service Menu Mastery
Learn every service offering — the exit assessment, value acceleration program, exit readiness score, and engagement structures.
Competitor Landscape
Research the Charlotte market. Understand how Exit Factor differentiates from M&A advisors, brokers, and generalist consultants.
Week 2
Foundation: Know Your Market
Exit Planning Industry 101
Read IBBA and BEI primers on exit planning. Understand the $10T wealth transfer wave driving this market.
The Value Gap
Study the difference between what a business is worth today vs. what the owner needs for financial independence.
Charlotte Market Research
Research businesses in Mecklenburg, Gaston, Union, and York counties with 10–200 employees. Know your primary prospect industries.
Referral Partner Landscape
Map the ecosystem: CPAs, financial advisors, wealth managers, estate attorneys, commercial bankers, and business brokers.

4-Week Pre-Training Ramp Up
EXIT FACTOR
Week 3
Messaging & Tools
Your Elevator Pitch
Draft and practice your 30-second and 2-minute pitches. Record yourself. Refine until it's natural and confident.
LinkedIn Profile Optimization
Update your headline, about section, and experience. Add the Exit Factor affiliation. Begin connecting with 10 targeted people per day.
CRM Setup
Get set up in the CRM. Begin entering your List of 100 contacts and tagging by type: referral partner, prospect, or community connector.
Digital Asset Review
Review all Exit Factor marketing collateral, one-pagers, email templates, and presentation decks. Know what's available and when to use each.
Shadow a Discovery Call
Sit in (live or recorded) on at least one Discovery Call or prospect presentation. Take notes on objections, questions, and flow.
Week 4
Network Activation
List of 100 Completion
Finalize your full list. Prioritize the top 20 as Tier 1 outreach. Begin scheduling meetings before training.
Referral Group Identification
Identify 3–5 BNI chapters or referral groups in your geography. Attend at least one as a visitor this week.
Chamber Registration
Join the Charlotte Chamber or local equivalent. Register for at least 2 events before your training date.
Initial Outreach Blitz
Send personalized messages to your top 20 Tier 1 contacts. Plant the seed and schedule a 1:1 before training.
Training Prep Review
Review all pre-work assigned by corporate. Arrive prepared with questions ready, not just present.
